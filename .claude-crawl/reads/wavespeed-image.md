## How to Generate AI Images [Permalink for this section](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/image-generator\#how-to-generate-ai-images)

1. [Sign up](https://wavespeed.ai/sign-in) for a free WaveSpeedAI account (Google or GitHub)
2. Go to [WaveSpeedAI Image Generator](https://wavespeed.ai/image-generator)
3. Select an image model from the curated list
4. Enter a detailed text prompt describing your desired image
5. Adjust parameters — size, style, number of images
6. Click **Run** to generate

> **Tip:** Use the **Prompt Enhancer** to automatically improve your prompt for higher quality results.

> **Important:** Generated images are retained for **7 days**. Download any files you want to keep before they expire.

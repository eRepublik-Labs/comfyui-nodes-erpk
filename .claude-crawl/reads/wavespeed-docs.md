# WaveSpeedAI Documentation

WaveSpeedAI provides unified API access to 700+ AI models for text-to-image,
image-to-video, text-to-video, and audio generation. Supported models include
FLUX, Kling, Veo, Luma, Stable Diffusion, Seedance, Minimax, and more.

**Get started with WaveSpeedAI:**

- **[Image Generator](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/image-generator)** — Curated AI image models, no coding required
- **[Video Generator](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/video-generator)** — Curated AI video models, no coding required
- **[API Integration](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/rest-api)** — RESTful APIs for developers
- **[Desktop App](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/desktop-app)** — Native apps for Windows, macOS, and Linux
- **[Python SDK](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/python-sdk)** — Simple Python library
- **[JavaScript SDK](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/javascript-sdk)** — Node.js and browser library
- **[ComfyUI](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/comfyui-integration)** — Node-based workflow integration
- **[N8N](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/n8n-integration)** — No-code automation workflows
- **[Web Interface](https://wavespeed.ai/)** — Browser-based, no coding required

## Account Levels [Permalink for this section](https://wavespeed.ai/docs\#account-levels)

WaveSpeedAI offers four account levels to suit different usage requirements:

| Level | Images/min | Videos/min | Max Concurrent Tasks | Activation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze | 10 | 5 | 3 | Default for new users |
| Silver | 500 | 60 | 100 | One-time top-up of $100 |
| Gold | 3,000 | 600 | 2,000 | One-time top-up of $1,000 |
| Ultra | 5,000 | 5,000 | 5,000 | One-time top-up of $10,000 |

**Bronze:** Default level for new users. New accounts receive $1 trial credit — please note that some models are not available with trial credit.

**Silver:** One-time top-up of $100 upgrades your account to Silver.

**Gold:** One-time top-up of $1,000 upgrades your account to Gold.

**Ultra:** One-time top-up of $10,000 upgrades your account to Ultra.

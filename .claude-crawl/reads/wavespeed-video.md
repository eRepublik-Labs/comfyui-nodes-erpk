## How to Generate AI Videos [Permalink for this section](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/video-generator\#how-to-generate-ai-videos)

1. [Sign up](https://wavespeed.ai/sign-in) for a free WaveSpeedAI account (Google or GitHub)
2. Go to [WaveSpeedAI Video Generator](https://wavespeed.ai/video-generator)
3. Select a video model from the curated list
4. Enter a text prompt or upload a reference image
5. Set parameters — duration, aspect ratio, motion intensity
6. Click **Run** to generate your video

> **Tip:** For best results with **Image to Video**, use a high-resolution source image and describe the desired motion in your prompt.

> **Important:** Generated videos are retained for **7 days**. Download any files you want to keep before they expire.

## FAQ [Permalink for this section](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/video-generator\#faq)

### Is WaveSpeedAI Video Generator free to use? [Permalink for this section](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/video-generator\#is-wavespeedai-video-generator-free-to-use)

Yes, the Video Generator tool is completely free to use. You’ll receive free credits when you sign up — no credit card required. Once your free credits are used up, you can top up your account to continue generating.

### How long does it take to generate a video? [Permalink for this section](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/video-generator\#how-long-does-it-take-to-generate-a-video)

Video generation typically takes 30 seconds to a few minutes depending on the model, resolution, and duration. This is longer than image generation due to the complexity of video processing.

### Can I use the generated videos commercially? [Permalink for this section](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/video-generator\#can-i-use-the-generated-videos-commercially)

In general, yes. However, each model may have its own license terms. Please review the individual model’s documentation for specific usage rights.

### What video formats are supported? [Permalink for this section](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/video-generator\#what-video-formats-are-supported)

Generated videos are typically output in MP4 format.

### Do all models support audio? [Permalink for this section](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/video-generator\#do-all-models-support-audio)

No. Audio generation is available on select models such as Seedance 1.5 Pro. Check the model description on the Video Generator page to see which models include audio.

### How is this different from the Models page? [Permalink for this section](https://wavespeed.ai/docs/video-generator\#how-is-this-different-from-the-models-page)

The [Models](https://wavespeed.ai/models) page gives you access to 700+ AI models with full parameter control. The Video Generator is a simplified, curated experience with hand-picked models — perfect if you’re new or just want quick results.

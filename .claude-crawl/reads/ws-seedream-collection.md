## Seedream Models

Seedream is **ByteDance's next-generation AI image generation and editing suite**, designed for everything from photorealistic portraits and illustrations to brand key visuals, posters, and e-commerce creatives. The flagship **Seedream 5.0** architecture unifies text-to-image and image-to-image in one powerful stack, delivering sharper details, more stable compositions, and stronger prompt adherence than earlier models. It easily outperforms older image models such as Nano Banana, especially for 4K-ready ads, cinematic key art, and high-conversion marketing images.
